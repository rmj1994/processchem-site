ProcessChem Documentation

About

ProcessChem is a chemical process engineering calculator for your phone. This wiki is its documentation: one page for each tab, Thermo mode and Case, and a few pages on using the app.

The Docs ↗ button at the top of every screen opens the matching page here in your browser: the tab's page on a tab's home screen, and the mode's or case's own page once you have chosen one. The ⓘ beside a value in the app opens a sheet on where that value came from, and the ⋯ menu beside Docs ↗ opens, saves and resets your inputs.

Every page follows the same shape: About first, then the inputs, what comes back, and the physics behind it.

Tabs

  • Property — look up one chemical or mixture's properties at a temperature and pressure
  • Thermo — four ways of asking what happens to a substance
  • Case — real-world scenarios, each with its own inputs
  • Units — per-quantity unit choice, presets, a unit converter and the Normal-flow reference

Using the app

  • Getting around — the tabs, the inputs and results screens, and the buttons every screen shares
  • Saving and opening — what the ⋯ menu keeps, and how to save a case to a file that survives deleting the app
  • Methods and data sources — where each number comes from, and how to change the method behind it

Thermo modes

  • Phase Equilibrium — one chemical changing state
  • Chemical Equilibrium — a feed left to find its own minimum
  • Reaction — a reaction you already know
  • Electrochemical Reaction — a reaction driven by electricity, or driving it

Cases

  • Heater — heats or cools a batch or stream at constant pressure
  • Vessel Heater — a sealed rigid vessel heated or cooled
  • Fluidized Bed — sizes a fluidized bed
  • Heat Exchanger — two streams exchanging heat
